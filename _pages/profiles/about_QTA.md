# Quinn Anderson

### Research Associate

Quinn received his bachelor’s degree in biology from Taylor University in 2021 and graduated with his MS degree in Biology and certificate in Biotechnology from Ball State University in 2023. After becoming married in July, Quinn joined the lab as a Research Associate in August of 2023. He helped to establish a consistent behavioral testing regiment while refining lab protocols such as the Western blot and Golgi-Cox staining. In addition, Quinn has sustained a project that has shown the efficacy of P7C3-A20 in C9orf72 ALS/FTD murine models. Outside of the lab, he enjoys hiking, biking, kayaking, and anything else outdoors.
