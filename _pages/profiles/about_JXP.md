# Jinxiu Pan

### [placeholder title]

Jinxiu received her medical degree from Nantong University in China in 2011 and a master’s degree in Rheumatology from Tongji Medical College in 2014. She came to the United States to pursue biomedical research and worked in Dr. Xiong’s lab at Augusta University (2014–2017) and Case Western Reserve University (2017–2022), studying bone biology and the interactions between bone, muscle, and the central nervous system. From 2022 to 2023, she studied epigenetic regulation of steroid signaling in prostate cancer in Dr. Sharifi’s lab at Cleveland Clinic. From 2023 to 2026, Jinxiu worked in Dr. Skowronski’s lab at Case Western Reserve University, using stop-codon suppression technology to label HIV-1 particles. In June 2026, she joined Dr. Burberry’s lab, where she currently studies how loss of C9orf72 contributes to ALS.
